		<p>There will be a DDD (Developer! Developer! Developer!) style event in Scotland some time soon. In other words it would be on a Saturday. It would last the whole day. There would be different tracks so you could pick and choose what you wanted to see. The atmosphere would be great!</p>
<p>There have now been 4 very successful developer days held in Reading and a fifth one on the way. I can't wait to get down there, but the travel is a bit of a hassle. It is over 400 miles. If it was closer to home it would be much better.</p>
<p>So, the call is out! If you are interested in a <a title="Developer Day" target="_blank" href="http://www.developerday.co.uk/">Developer Day</a> Scotland then respond to this thread on the <a title="Scottish Developers" target="_blank" href="http://www.scottishdevelopers.com/">Scottish Developers</a> website: <font face="Arial"><a href="http://www.scottishdevelopers.com/modules/newbb/viewtopic.php?viewmode=flat&amp;topic_id=848&amp;forum=1100">http://www.scottishdevelopers.com/modules/newbb/viewtopic.php?viewmode=flat&amp;topic_id=848&amp;forum=1100</a> </font></p>
<p>Alternatively, drop us an email at <strong>dds AT scottishdevelopers DOT com</strong></p>
<p>The current ideas floating around would be to do this three months out of phase with the <a title="Developer Day" target="_blank" href="http://www.developerday.co.uk/">Developer Day</a> held at TVP in Reading so they didn't clash. It would also give people the opportunity to attend both if they wanted - There are a fair few people from Scotland travelling down to Reading for the existing DDD, so why not have some folk from the south of England travelling north to Scotland too?</p>
<p>At the moment we are looking out for sponsors to help pay for it all. We would like to keep it free if we can. </p>
<p>So, get the message out. There will be a <a title="Developer Day" target="_blank" href="http://www.developerday.co.uk/">Developer Day</a> in Scotland! And you're all invited.</p>
