		<p><strong><u>WARNING: Incoming rant.</u></strong></p>
<p><font class="messagecontent">As you know, I help out on forums from time-to-time... Well, a lot actually. But sometimes people just don't seem to want to give up the most basic of information which might help diagnose the problem. </font></p>
<p><font class="messagecontent">I've <a href="http://blogs.wdevs.com/ColinAngusMackay/archive/2006/05/04/13024.aspx">blogged about this in the past</a> and this example, especially the response to my initial question, shows the utter uselessness of the information given.<br />
<br />
Eejit: My stored procedure doesn't work. It has an error.<br />
(A stored procedure was listed with no obvious point of failure)</font><font class="messagecontent"><br />
Me: What is the error?<br />
Eejit: The error code is not zero but another number.<br />
(Note: Zero = success, an other number indicates various types of error)<br />
Me: What is the error code given?<br />
<br />
At this point I just want to give up in dispair. Do these people go to their doctor and say simply: "I feel unwell. It hurts." And then where the doctor asks where they respond: "Somewhere on my body." Do they expect their doctor to diagnose them with that level of information. Maybe they should have seen a vet - at least then they could have been put out of their misery! </font></p>
