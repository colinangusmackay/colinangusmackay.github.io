		<p>Since announcing my little application to help make technorati tags easier for my blog posts I've gone and fixed a couple of bugs. I suppose that's what you get for writing an application in a few minutes - not enough time for testing.</p>
<p>So, now that non-alphanumeric characters are encoded properly and empty tags (caused by a trailing comma, or no tag between commas) are ignored <a href="http://www.colinmackay.net/tabid/157/Default.aspx">the new version of the Technorati Tag Builder</a> is available on my website. Also, for those that just want a simple life, an installer is now available.</p>
