I <a href="http://www.joelonsoftware.com/articles/fog0000000043.html">just read</a> this article by Joel spolsky on <strong>12 steps to better code</strong>. I particularly like item 7: Do you have a spec? Don't make me laugh - Do I have a spec? Ha, ha, haa!

Item 9 also contains a very amusing story:
<blockquote>At <a href="http://www.joelonsoftware.com/articles/TwoStories.html">my last job</a>, the system administrator kept sending me automated spam complaining that I was using more than ... get this ... 220 megabytes of hard drive space on the server. I pointed out that given the price of hard drives these days, the cost of this space was significantly less than the cost of the <em>toilet paper</em> I used. Spending even 10 minutes cleaning up my directory would be a fabulous waste of productivity.</blockquote>
