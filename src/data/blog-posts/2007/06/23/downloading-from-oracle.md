		I'm currently downloading <a href="http://www.oracle.com/technology/products/database/xe/index.html">Oracle Database 10g Express Edition</a> and I just got the oddest email from Oracle themselves. In order to download the product, I had to register on their website. There were the usual set of check boxes for various newsletters that may interest me. I ensured they were all blank. And I signed up. I'm currently downloading the 157Mb download and then my email pops up to tell me I've got a message. It was Oracle to tell me:<br />
<!--StartFragment --> 
<div class="moz-text-html" lang="x-unicode">
<pre><font face="Arial">We have received a request to unsubscribe the following e-mail address, <br /><strong><a href="mailto:XXXXXXXXXXX@YAHOO.CO.UK,from">XXXXXXXXXXX@YAHOO.CO.UK,</a> from Oracle e-mail communications and <br />subscriptions.

If you did not request to be removed or do not wish to unsubscribe please
</strong></font><a href="http://blog.colinmackay.net/Providers/BlogEntryEditor/FCKeditor/editor/"><font face="Arial">click here</font></a><font face="Arial"> to update your communication preferences.*

*Please note, as an Oracle customer or Partner you may continue to receive
e-mail communications containing business critical information.</font>
</pre>
</div>
<p>I don't remember signing up for anything. But I'm pretty pleased that I'm unsubscribed from the newsletters I don't remember signing up for.</p>
<p>I suspect Oracle really need to do a check before sending this email out to see if the person is actually subscribed to anything in the first place. And if not, then don't bother with this email.</p>
