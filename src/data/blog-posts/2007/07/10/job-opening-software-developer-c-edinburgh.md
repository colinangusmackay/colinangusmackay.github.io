		<p><span class="text"><em><strong>Submissions are now CLOSED</strong></em></span></p>
<p><span class="text"><em>I just got the following from a friend. His company are looking to hire a software developer. If you are interested and fit the bill then please let him know:</em><br />
<br />
Company: <strong>Pilgrim Systems</strong><br />
Job Title: <strong>SOFTWARE DEVELOPER<br />
</strong>Description: Are you a real programmer looking for a proper development job? If so, read on. As Joel Spolsky says “The most satisfying careers, if you’re a software developer, are at actual software companies, not doing IT for some bank”. <br />
<br />
We’re looking for experienced software developers to assist in the design and development of our new suite of software tools for the legal sector. <br />
<br />
Due to the ongoing evolution of our legal software you will be at the forefront of developing the next generation of LawSoft software, utilising the technologies such as .Net, C# and XML. In addition, you will be involved in delivering software enhancements to meet specific client needs. <br />
<br />
Your main responsibilities will include:- <br />
<br />
· Developing &amp; maintenance of code that adheres to the Company coding standards <br />
· Production of high quality software <br />
· Writing specifications and estimating development project timescales and milestones <br />
<br />
Candidates must have 3 years work experience in the following: <br />
<strong>· C# or VC++ <br />
· SQL <br />
· ASP.NET &amp; ASP <br />
· Object-oriented design</strong> <br />
<br />
The following skills are desirable: <br />
· XML/XSL <br />
· MFC <br />
· Microsoft Office Development <br />
<br />
You must have the following: <br />
· Good interpersonal skills <br />
· Educated to degree or similar qualification <br />
· Ability to communicate well both orally and in writing <br />
· Ability and interest in learning new technologies <br />
· A demonstrable interest in a career in software development <br />
· Team player <br />
<br />
Closing Date 23rd July 2007 <br />
<br />
Negotiable salary and benefits package <br />
<br />
If you are interested in the above position and would like to be part of a growing company please email your CV to <a href="mailto:jobs@pilgrimsystems.com">jobs@pilgrimsystems.com</a> quoting the above reference. <br />
<br />
About Pilgrim Systems <br />
<br />
Pilgrim Systems is an Edinburgh based software house developing complete IT solutions for law firms. Founded in 1978 the company has established a dominant position in the Scottish legal market and has become a leading supplier of legal IT systems in England and Wales.</span></p>
