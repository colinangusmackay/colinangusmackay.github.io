		<p>As I've mentioned before I'm using <a title="Google" href="http://www.google.co.uk" target="_blank">Google</a> Analytics to see what people are
interested in on my blog and website. Some of the statistics are beginning to
settle down now. So, here are some interesting stats (well, they are interesting
to me)</p>
<p>Most read blog entry was about installing Visual Studio 2005 on Vista</p>
<p>Top three browsers are IE (69%), Firefox (28%) and Opera (2%)</p>
<p>97% of visitors use Windows, 2% use an Apple Macintosh.</p>
<p>95% of visitors have their screen resolution set at 1024x768 or higher. The
most common setting is 1280x1024 which accounts for 29% of visitors.</p>
<p>1% of visitors came from an IP address registered to Microsoft Corp.</p>
