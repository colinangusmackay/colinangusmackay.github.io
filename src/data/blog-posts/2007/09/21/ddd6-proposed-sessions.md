		<p>It is now possible to <a href="http://www.developerday.co.uk/ddd/agendaddd6.asp">propose a session for DDD6</a>. It has only been open for a few hours and already there are a few good sessions available. Now I'm in a quandry. If this is the quality of all the sessions I'll have no idea what to see. Not to mention that I'll be submitting my own session idea. </p>
<p>So, if you have a session idea then get over to the DDD website and <a href="http://www.developerday.co.uk/ddd/editsession.asp">submit your session idea</a>.</p>
