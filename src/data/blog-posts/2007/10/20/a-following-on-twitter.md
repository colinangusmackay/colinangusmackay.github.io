		<p>I've noticed that I have about double the number of followers than those that I am following. I'm not entirely sure what that means. I'm only following people that I've actually met. Does that mean I say interesting things, or are people just randomly following in the hope that I'll follow them back. And if it is the latter then what do they get out of it? (Especially if I don't know them)</p>  <p>If I look at most of the tweets that I make I would say that the majority would only be relevant to people that I know. Most of the rest are notifications of new blog posts and such (which can be received from an RSS feed anyway) So I wonder why others are following me.</p>  <p>So, a request: If you follow me on Twitter and I don't (yet) follow you back, then would you be so kind as to let me know why? I'm always curious about these things.
