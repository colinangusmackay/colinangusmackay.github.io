		<p>Some great news for user groups is that <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/en/us/default.aspx">Microsoft</a> are picking up feeds from <a href="http://www.developerfusion.co.uk/">Developer Fusion</a> to put a feed of UK community events up in the <a href="http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-gb/aa497440.aspx">Community section</a> of the <a href="http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-gb/default.aspx">MSDN website</a>. This will give user groups much more exposure to their target audience and this will hopefully result in better events as more people become involved.</p>
