<a href="http://www.jetbrains.com/" target="_blank">JetBrains</a>, the makers of <a href="http://www.jetbrains.com/resharper/" target="_blank">ReSharper</a> and <a href="http://www.jetbrains.com/profiler/" target="_blank">dotTrace</a>, have sponsored <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Default.aspx" target="_blank">Developer Day Scotland</a> with 6 bundles of ReSharper and dotTrace to give away as prizes. That's $550 worth of software to each lucky winner.

Developer Day Scotland, based on the highly successful Developer! Developer! Developer! events, is being held on 10th May 2008 in Glasgow.

The <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Agenda/tabid/55/Default.aspx" target="_blank">call for speakers</a> is still open, but hurry, it will be closing soon!
