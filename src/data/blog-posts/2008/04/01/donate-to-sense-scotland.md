		<p>A friend of mine, Martin Bell, is going to do a crazy thing for charity to help raise money for <a href="http://www.sensescotland.org.uk/index.php" target="_blank">Sense Scotland</a> right after <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Default.aspx" target="_blank">Developer Day Scotland</a>. Sense Scotland is a charity that works with children and adults who have communication support needs because of deaf-blindness, sensory impairment, learning and physical disabilities. It is an excellent cause and the money will be put to very good use.</p>  <p>If you want to sponsor him then you can go to his <a href="https://sensescotland.workwithus.org/Fundraising/Donate.aspx?page=2173" target="_blank">sponsorship page</a> and donate what ever you feel is appropriate.
