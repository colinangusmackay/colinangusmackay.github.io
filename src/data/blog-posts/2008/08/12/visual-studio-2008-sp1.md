		<p>Visual Studio 2008 SP1 is here and can be downloaded. <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/charlie/archive/2008/08/11/released-visual-studio-service-pack-1-net-3-5-service-pack-1.aspx">Details of the downloads are here</a>, and <a href="http://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/vstudio/products/cc533447.aspx">information on what SP1 brings is here</a>. The big items for me are the Entity Framework and the performance improvements in LINQ.</p>  <p>However, before you go rushing off to install SP1 there are some caveats.
