Some people though, crave more control. They want to operate that bit closer to the metal, enjoy a clear separation of concerns and ensure that unit testing their application is made as easy as possible. </p>  <p>Enter <a href="http://www.asp.net/mvc/">ASP.NET MVC</a>. ASP.NET MVC enables you to build <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model-view-controller">Model View Controller (MVC)</a> applications with the ASP.NET framework as an alternative to ASP.NET webforms. </p>  <p>In this session we'll take a look at the drivers behind MVC, the architecture of a typical ASP.NET MVC application, new features in ASP.NET that make it possible and we'll build a simple ASP.NET MVC application to explore the key concepts. </p>  <p><strong>Speaker Bio</strong></p>  <p>Mike Ormond gained a BSc(Hons) in Information Engineering from the University of Strathclyde before moving south of the border many years ago.
