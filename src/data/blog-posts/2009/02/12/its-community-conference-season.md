		<p>Community conference season is on us. What ever you do there is a conference to suit you somewhere in the UK in the next few months. The only problem you are going to have is fitting it all in.</p>  <p>Up first is <a href="http://www.sqlbits.com/">SQL Bits IV</a> on 28th March 2009 in Manchester. The <a href="http://www.sqlbits.com/information/Registration.aspx">delegate registration is now open</a> - so get 'em while they're hot!</p>  <p>Then on 4th April the community heads over to Belfast for <a href="http://www.developerdeveloperdeveloper.com/belfast/">DDD Belfast</a>, the first time the DDD conference has visited Northern Ireland. <a href="http://www.developerdeveloperdeveloper.com/belfast/Users/VoteForSessions.aspx">Voting is currently open</a> for that conference.</p>  <p>Then in May you have two choices, one at each end of the UK! On the 2nd May in Glasgow there is <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Default.aspx">Developer Day Scotland</a> where <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Voting/tabid/81/Default.aspx">voting has recently opened</a>. Then in Taunton there is <a href="http://www.dddsouthwest.com/">DDD SouthWest</a>, where the <a href="http://www.dddsouthwest.com/CallForNewSpeakers/tabid/61/Default.aspx">Call for Speakers</a> is currently open.</p>  <p>There is lots of choice and the diverse range of locations means that you shouldn't have to travel far to find something near you. If you are very keen it also means an opportunity to visit lots of places you may not have been before. I'm particularly looking forward to Taunton - I've never been down that way before!</p>  <p><strong><font color="#ff0000">UPDATE!</font></strong></p>  <p>The community conferences just keep on coming. We really are spoiled for choice this year! Two more conferences announced, both on the 18th April!</p>  <ul>   <li><a href="http://www.altdotnetuknorth.info/">ALT.NET 'in the North' conference</a> (Bradford)</li>    <li><a href="http://www.developerdeveloperdeveloper.com/webdd09/">WebDD'09</a> (Reading)</li> </ul>
