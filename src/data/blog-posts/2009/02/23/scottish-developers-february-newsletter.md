		<h1>Welcome</h1>  <p>Last week in the pub after an event I was asked why there wasn't much non-Microsoft stuff at Scottish Developers. Fair question. We are after-all supposed to be here to cater for the entire developer community, not just one section of it. The reality is that the current committee mostly use Microsoft tools and the areas in which we happen to operate on a daily basis are within that sphere of influence. It therefore becomes easy for us just to invite speakers that we already know. Not a very good reason, and really rather a poor excuse too. But, I want to change that.</p>  <p>I would like to invite anyone who knows of an event that would be of interest to folks involved with non-Microsoft developer technologies to let me know and I'll add it to the events page and announce it on the website. If you want to get more involved, such as being able to speak at an event, then we would be very interested in that too. Finally, we always welcome feedback, so if you have any other ideas then let me know. We already do joint events from time-to-time with the SQL Server User Group, so why not joint events with other organisations. All feedback, comments, suggestions, etc. can be sent to <a href="mailto:support@scottishdevelopers.com">support@scottishdevelopers.com</a> </p>  <p><a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Default.aspx"><img style="margin-left:15px;margin-right:10px;border-width:0;" height="86" alt="Developer Day Scotland - 2nd May 2009" src="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Portals/0/ddds-logo-banner.gif" width="170" align="right" border="0"></a>Things are now moving forward quite well with <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/Default.aspx">Developer Day Scotland 2009</a>. The session voting is currently open, but hurry, it closes at the end of the month. I hope you will take the opportunity to vote for the sessions that you want. It is a tough choice as we have so many <a href="http://developerdayscotland.com/main/SessionProposals/tabid/77/Default.aspx">excellent sessions</a>. The delegate registration will open early during the first week of March, so keep an eye on the website for details.</p>  <p>As I mentioned above, we are always keen to find out what you want, so if you have any suggestions then please let us know. You can email event suggestions to <a href="mailto:support@scottishdevelopers.com">support@scottishdevelopers.com</a> </p>  <p>Did you know we have a twitter feed? You can follow us <a href="http://twitter.com/scottishdevs">@scottishdevs</a>. </p>  <p>As always, we are on the look out for new speakers.
