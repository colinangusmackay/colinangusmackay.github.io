<a href="https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/3863">iMicros</a> is a plug in for <a href="http://www.mozilla-europe.org/en/firefox/">FireFox</a> that lets you automate repetitive tasks. This is a fantastic tool for anyone that develops websites as the flow through the site can be recorded and played back quickly and repeatedly. It reduces tester error by taking the mundane parts out of performing user tests. It can also deal with AJAX, has a stopwatch so you can time page loads, and many other things.
