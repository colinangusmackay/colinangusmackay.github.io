<p><a href="http://www.dddsouthwest.com/"><img style="background-image:none;margin:0 5px;padding-left:0;padding-right:0;display:inline;float:right;padding-top:0;border-width:0;" border="0" align="right" src="http://www.dddsouthwest.com/images/DDDSouthWest3BadgeLarge.png" width="192" height="254" /></a>I’ll be speaking at <a href="http://www.dddsouthwest.com" target="_blank">DDD South West</a> 3 this year. My talk on Parallelisation (“<em>Parallel... Parallelise... Pallar... Doing stuff at the same time in .NET 4.0</em>”) was voted onto the agenda.</p>  <blockquote>   <p>.NET 4 has some very nifty features to aid the creation of multiple threads of operation. In this talk I'll be introducing those features and demonstrating what they can do for you, how you can benefit... And importantly, what you shouldn't do if you want to maintain your sanity. I'll also be doing my best work out how to correctly pronounce &quot;parallelisation&quot; by DDD South West.</p> </blockquote>  <p>If you are interested in an introduction to Parallelisation in .NET 4 then it would be a great opportunity to learn more about it.</p>  <p>Incidentally, if you can’t make it to Bristol I’ll be doing the talk at <a href="http://www.developerdeveloperdeveloper.com/scotland2011/" target="_blank">DDD Scotland 2011</a> in May. And if you can’t wait at all then just check out the <a title="Blog posts on the topic of Parallelisation" href="http://colinmackay.co.uk/blog/tag/parallelisation/" target="_blank">Parallelisation</a> tag on my blog for a number of articles on the subject or the <a title="Scottish Developers Parallelisation Talk Overview" href="http://colinmackay.co.uk/blog/2011/04/21/scottish-developers-parallelisation-talk-overview/" target="_blank">overview from the talk</a> I did at <a title="Scottish Developers" href="http://scottishdevelopers.com">Scottish Developers</a> last night.</p>
