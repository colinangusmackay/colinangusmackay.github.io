When you can download lots of DVD and CD images from likes of MSDN it can become rather annoying having to burn ISO images to physical CDs or DVDs. However, <a href="http://www.slysoft.com/en/virtual-clonedrive.html">Virtual Clone Drive</a> mounts an ISO image as a virtual drive so you don't have to burn physical drives. This saves on the cost of physical disks and once mounted will typically run faster than a CD or DVD drive.
