<a href="http://colinmackay.co.uk/2012/annual-report/"><img alt="" src="http://www.wordpress.com/wp-content/mu-plugins/annual-reports/img/2012-emailteaser.png" width="100%" /></a>

Wordpress emailed me a very nice summary of my blog stats for 2012. Actually, they emailed me twice because at the start of 2012 I self-hosted then I moved to WordPress when I realised that would be much cheaper and take a lot less effort. The only downside being that I had to give up Google Analytics. Below is a bit of a summary, I've tried to combine the numbers from both sides of the fence to give a more accurate picture:

&nbsp;
<blockquote>4,329 films were submitted to the 2012 Cannes Film Festival. This blog had <strong>103,000</strong> views in 2012. If each view were a film, this blog would power 24 Film Festivals.In 2012, there were <strong>36</strong> new posts, growing the total archive of this blog to 492 posts.
