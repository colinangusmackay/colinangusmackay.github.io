<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I don't use the built in source control plug in Visual Studio as I use <a href="https://www.gitkraken.com/">GitKraken</a> instead, so Visual Studio's plug-in just sat in the background not doing much as far as I could see.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then out-of-the-blue I got a notification that it was slowing down Visual Studio and I should turn it off if I don't rely on it. Fair enough, I don't use it, it can go.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Open the Options dialog</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the options dialog by going to the Tools→Options... menu item</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":13745,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://colinmackay.scot/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image.png"><img src="https://colinmackay.scot/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image.png?w=467" alt="" class="wp-image-13745" /></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Find the Source Control Plug in Section</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Type <code>Source Control Plug-in Selection</code> in the search box and press enter</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":13747,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://colinmackay.scot/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-1.png"><img src="https://colinmackay.scot/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-1.png?w=749" alt="" class="wp-image-13747" /></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Change the drop down to <code>None</code> and then press <code>OK</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you have a solution open, it will likely tell you it has to close the solution.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That's it!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
